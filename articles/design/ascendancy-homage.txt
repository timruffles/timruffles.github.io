Building a Homage to Ascendancy

I'm building a remake of a game that fascinated me from the first time I saw it with its unique look, feel and sound: Ascendancy. Take a look at this:


What I think stands up even today is the art direction, the decision to show you how a planet feels rather than turning it into a spreadsheet. If it's flourishing, there's a lush growth in the prosperity section.

The design choices of the game were great throughout, but limited by the resources of the time. The amibituous 3D galaxy and rotating star-systems feel hard to use these days with the on-screen controls. Micromanagement made the later stages of the game drag, Also, the AI was famously feedble, even with the (excellently named) Antagonizer patch.


Therefore I think the base game and feel deserves a reimagining with modern rendering. So I'm building a homage to the game - the same rules, and taking the art direction from the original as far as I can. I want this to feel like Ascendancy, while looking better and being more fun to play.

Here's where I am so far. I'm not hand-coding this, which is helping me stay at the design level. I'm a month in so far, with a largely asynchronous process of a batch of prompts before and after work, with the agent working all day. I've been using a Codex $200/month subscription, and thanks to the release of Terra and some resets, have been able to keep it running pretty well constantly. It has been interesting to have to lean so far into automony, and I'll share some of the techniques I've been using to avoid needing close supervision.


This is intended to be a gift to fellow fans, and perhaps share some of the original magic of the game to people who've not tried it. So I've no plans to monetise this - I'm paying forward the gift the original team gave me by putting this weird game out into the world.
